The lighthouse lamp is copper. The harbor bell is bronze. The market bell is dawn.
The ferry departure is dusk. The baker's loaf is fig bread. The dock rope is coiled hemp.

The salt cargo is linen sacks. The captain is Ivo. The map-room key is brass.
The turning tide is the ninth bell. Boats leave only after the harbor bell.

Ivo keeps the harbor watch and checks the map-room key before dusk.
The lighthouse lamp is copper, and sailors use it to find the coiled hemp.
