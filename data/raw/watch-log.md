Watch log, ninth bell.

The turning tide is the ninth bell. Ivo walks the dock and finds the dock rope is coiled hemp.
The baker's loaf is fig bread, set out when the market bell is dawn.
The lighthouse lamp is copper and is lit before the ferry departure is dusk.
The salt cargo is linen sacks stacked beside the harbor bell, which is bronze.
The map-room key is brass and stays with the captain, who is Ivo.
